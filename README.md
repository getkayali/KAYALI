KAYALI is a maps and navigation app for Syria that works on your phone without an internet connection and without an account. The maps, search and routes are stored in the app itself.

What KAYALI offers:
- Maps of Syria and south Türkiye (Saudi Arabia included)and place search, offline.
- Spoken guidance before each turn, by car or on foot, with a new route if you leave it.
- Several places in one trip: pick the shops, note what you need at each, and get a suggested order.
- Drawn routes, or routes received on WhatsApp: KAYALI guides you to the start, then along the route.
- Emergency: an "I need help" message with your location and medical card, plus nearby help (approximate; places may change).
- Danger areas: mark and share them; routes avoid them and you are warned as you approach.
- KAYALI Code: a short code to share any place in Syria.
- Larger and bolder text for easier reading.
- Arabic, English and Turkish.

Your privacy: KAYALI sends nothing by itself. Every message is one you choose to send.

To be clear: some features are trials, and work continues. Map data can be incomplete or out of date, so always follow road signs. The app is large because the maps are built in.
